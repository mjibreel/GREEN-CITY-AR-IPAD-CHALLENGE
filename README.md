Project Overview:
The Green City AR iPad Challenge is an innovative project that showcases a sustainable city model using augmented reality (AR). This project was developed as part of the "Shaping Sustainable Urban Cities" initiative held from October 2023 to January 2024. The challenge aimed to highlight eco-friendly urban planning concepts, including renewable energy solutions, smart infrastructure, and sustainable living spaces.

Key Features:

🌿 Green Energy Solutions: Integration of wind turbines and solar-powered buildings.
🏢 Eco-Friendly Urban Zones: Divided into entertainment, business, educational, and residential areas to demonstrate optimized land use.
🚍 Smart Public Transport: Virtual representation of public transport routes to promote sustainable commuting.
🌱 Vertical Farming Zones: A conceptual design for reducing food miles and enhancing self-sufficient urban agriculture.
🌐 Interactive Experience: Designed to be experienced through AR-enabled iPads for a visually immersive presentation.
Project Highlights:

Awarded the Silver Award for exceptional design and sustainability concepts.
Demonstrates the potential of AR in city planning and public awareness campaigns.
Purpose:
This project aims to inspire communities and city planners to adopt environmentally sustainable practices and to showcase how AR can transform the way urban projects are visualized and communicated.

Technologies Used:

3D Modeling Tools for creating detailed city elements.
Augmented Reality frameworks for immersive interaction.
iPad and Apple ecosystem for seamless AR presentation.
Contributors:
This project was developed as part of City University's collaborative initiative and was awarded recognition by UDA Holdings Berhad and UKM Pakarunding for its creativity and innovation.
